## Getting Started

### Logging In
Access the application at your shop's URL. Enter your **Email** and **Password** to sign in.

### Navigation
The sidebar on the left is your main command center.
- **Overview**: Dashboard.
- **Customers**: Manage client profiles.
- **Inventory**: Search parts and services.
- **Work Orders**: Manage service tickets.
- **Scheduling**: View calendar (coming soon).
- **Warranties**: Track active warranties.
- **Time Clock**: Clock in and out.

## Work Orders

The Work Orders tab is where service happens.

### Viewing Tickets
- **Rich List**: The main view shows all active tickets.
- **Search**: Use the search bar to find tickets by Customer Name or Ticket ID.
- **Filter**: Use the dropdown to filter by status (e.g., "Open", "Awaiting Parts").

### Creating a Work Order
1. Click **"New Work Order"** in the top navigation cards.
2. **Customer**: Click "Find Customer" to search or "New Customer" to create one.
3. **Bike**: Select the customer's bike from the dropdown, or enter details for a new one.
4. **Details**: Set the **Status**, **Assigned Tech**, and **Promised Date**.
5. **Add Items**: Use the "Items & Labor" section to search for parts or services and add them to the ticket.
6. Click **"Create Work Order"**.

### Editing a Work Order
Click on any row in the Work Order list to open the **Edit View**. You can update the status, add more parts, or change the internal notes.

## Customers

### Managing Profiles
- **Search**: Find customers by name, email, or phone.
- **Details**: Click a customer to view their profile, including contact info, bikes, and active warranties.
- **Edit**: Click "Edit" in the detail view to update their information.

### Registering a New Customer
You can create a new customer directly from the **Customers** tab or while creating a **Work Order**.

## Inventory

### Searching
Use the search bar in the **Inventory** tab to find parts. You can see:
- **Stock Levels**: Green badge = In Stock, Red badge = Low/Out of Stock.
- **Bin Location**: Where to find the item in the shop.
- **Pricing**: Retail price and cost.

### Editing Items
Click "View" on an item to see details. Click "Edit Item" to update stock quantities, pricing, or bin locations.

## Warranties

### Tracking Warranties
The **Warranties** tab shows a list of all active coverage.
- **Status Chips**: Quickly see if a warranty is `Active`, `Expired`, or `Void`.
- **Search**: Find warranties by Customer Name or Serial Number.

### Registering a Warranty
1. Click **"New Warranty"**.
2. **Customer**: Search for the customer by name.
3. **Template**: Select a warranty template (e.g., "WheelCare (12mo)").
4. **Details**: Enter the Purchase Date, Start Date, and Serial Number.
5. Click **"Register Warranty"**.

## Time Clock

### Clocking In/Out
1. Look for the **"IN/OUT"** chip in the sidebar.
2. Click the chip.
3. Enter your **4-digit Employee PIN** in the modal.
4. The system will confirm your clock-in or clock-out.

### Viewing the Roster
Click the arrow `▾` next to your name in the sidebar to see who is currently on shift ("On shift") and who is away ("Off shift").
